# atepdf: shared product-marketing context
**Current source: v6 handoff, 28 September 2026. Hypotheses are not results.**

## What we sell
A friendly one-screen PDF processor. Add files, describe the outcome and receive a real processed file. The interface removes tool hunting. Cloud AI understands the instruction; the user's browser or native app executes the document operations locally.

## First buyer and job
Initial paid hypothesis: US-English independent professionals, virtual assistants and small-business operators who repeatedly assemble document packs. Separate India founder-led pilots help usability; do not attribute them to US ads.

Hero job: combine files, make scanned pages searchable and compress towards a submission-size target. Use only capabilities verified on the target platform. The baseline claim is fewer decisions, not a measured universal speed improvement.

## Supported product boundary
Web, Android, iOS/iPadOS and native Mac are intended surfaces, released as verified. Windows/Linux are served through supported browsers, not native apps. No editing, annotation, forms, signatures, redaction, watermarks, content summarisation or high-fidelity Office conversion is promised.

Originals remain unchanged. Outputs are versioned. Follow-up instructions use the current result. Real errors and size limits are explained. No silent remote processing fallback.

## Offer hypotheses
India INR 249 monthly / 1,999 annual. International USD 5.99 monthly / 39.99 annual anchors. Free: useful local shortcuts and 3 completed AI jobs monthly. Pro: 300 completed intents per monthly window, supported larger batches and recipes. Provider-localised checkout is authoritative after approval. No trial by default, weekly-renewal anchor, lifetime unlimited AI or output hostage paywall. One entitlement is not document sync.
