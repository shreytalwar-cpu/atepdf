# Original UI references, retained unchanged

The approved iPhone/iPad and secondary desktop images in this folder are retained byte-for-byte from the prior pack. They define layout, interaction hierarchy, white/pastel styling and the secondary paper illustration.

Their **Attenlo PDF wordmark and A logo are superseded** by the final lowercase **atepdf** name and gradient **8** identity in [approved identity](../brand/APPROVED_BRAND.md).

The new brand board is authoritative for name/logo only. Ignore its product mock-ups, splash treatments, navigation and any incidental feature claims. Do not generate a new UI direction from it.

Build real controls, not screenshot backgrounds. No fonts, rejected concepts or compiled product binaries are supplied.
