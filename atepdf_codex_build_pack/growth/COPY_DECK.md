## Copy rules
Do not use "nothing ever leaves your device", "100% offline AI", "unlimited" for a limited plan, invented savings, fake stars, "free" for an immediate paid subscription or "cancel anytime" as a substitute for actual cancellation mechanics. Do not claim encryption/security certifications not implemented. Never present a disabled technical feature as a paid benefit. Use "Save" only for the action; show "Saved" only with evidence the platform provides.

Approved illustration stays. Avoid adding slogans to every corner, large competing CTAs or onboarding that makes a friendly utility look like a children's game.
