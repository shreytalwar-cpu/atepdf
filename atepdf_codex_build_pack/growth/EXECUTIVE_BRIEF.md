## Recommended first-use journey
Open directly to the workspace. Add files or choose a clearly labelled sample. Before the first remote instruction, explain that cloud AI sees the instruction and limited metadata while documents are processed locally, then request permission. Run actual engines. Return a real, validated file with preview/save/share. Keep the composer available for a follow-up. A declined permission leaves local shortcuts usable.

No required account, persona quiz, notification prompt or sales screen before value. A sample recipe is not a pretend live model call. Imported-file launches bypass unnecessary education. Decorative art collapses once the files are present.

## The commercial offer to test
Free: useful local shortcuts plus 3 successfully completed AI intents each month. Pro: 300 completed intents per monthly allowance window, supported larger batches and reusable recipes. India anchors: INR 249/month or INR 1,999/year; international anchors USD 5.99/month or USD 39.99/year. These remain hypotheses. Use verified localized provider prices at checkout, with actual full-period cost visible. Annual payment does not make the job allowance annual.

Launch without an auto-renewing trial. The free experience is the demonstration. A future trial or nonrenewing seven-day pass requires separate approval and economics. No weekly auto-renew default, lifetime unlimited AI, hidden free exit, fake savings, borrowed reviews or workspace ads.

The offer appears on explicit Pro intent or a genuine supported limit before starting a new job. Dismissal preserves files, draft and previously completed results. A paid user at the allowance cap sees the reset date, not another Buy Pro screen. Missing network, engine failure and unsupported files are not sales opportunities.

## The flows beyond onboarding and paywall
Purchase pending, cancelled and declined; server verification; restore/no-purchase/account conflicts; optional cross-device linking; duplicate subscription prevention; grace/hold/expiry/refund/revocation; cancelled renewal with remaining access; My plan; cancellation route; account deletion; consent withdrawal; local working-storage cleanup; missing recent file; failed/partial jobs; quota release; save/share cancellation; private diagnostics; optional recipe discovery and platform review prompts.

These are states of a small number of sheets and the main workspace, not 32 pages of onboarding. Clear recovery reduces support demand for a solo founder.

## Research changes what we should measure
Measure first valid result, real-file export initiation, repeat use, relevant offer exposure, verified first purchase, refund/renewal and contribution after acquisition. Synthetic-demo activity is separate. A browser download initiation is not proof that the user saved the file. Product analytics have a strict data allowlist with no document names/content, prompts or replay recordings.

Test one important commercial change at a time. Pricing and retention outputs remain hypotheses until actual cohorts mature. Thirty to fifty payers can guide the next test; that is not automatic statistical certainty. Revenue, advance cash and net contribution must remain separate.

## Economics carried forward, not silently changed
Original unit-cost estimate was INR 0.117 per AI job; the later growth model uses INR 0.1287 after adding supplier/FX contingency. Earlier and later subscriber profit targets differ because plan mix and reserves differ. Desired annual cash CAC targets of INR 350-900 are not validated by the later model's higher paid-channel CAC. Read `MODEL_RECONCILIATION.md` before using either spreadsheet.

The 36-month Base case still illustrates approximately 3,638 paying users and INR 4.94 lakh monthly operating profit at month 36, conditional on its assumptions. Nothing in the Appllama study upgrades that scenario to a forecast. No spending or live pricing is authorized by packaging these files.
