## Selected later scenario anchors, not targets promised to investors or customers
Base month 36: approximately 3,638 payers and INR 4.94 lakh operating profit/month; full third-year operating profit INR 39.06 lakh. Strong month 36: about 16,993 payers and INR 29.28 lakh/month. Downside never reaches profitability. Profit excludes income tax and founder compensation.

These outcomes depend on international mix, organic traffic growth, conversion, retention and acquisition cost assumptions. Appllama records do not validate any of them. Its app-level revenue/download metadata is not audited financial evidence and cannot establish that an onboarding flow caused commercial success. Do not reproduce it as atepdf forecasts or conversion benchmarks.
