# Creative playbook and first production batch
All copy is a draft. Do not publish until the advertised capability, actual footage, pricing, privacy wording and rights pass review.
Keep the approved atepdf identity. Product screen capture is the centrepiece; the mascot is a small brand accent.
The queue has 12 briefs, not a request to fund 12 ads simultaneously.

## First 3 creatives to produce
A01: one-instruction screen demonstration.
B01: document-pack problem/solution.
E02: founder explanation of local processing.
If OCR/compression is not yet release-ready, A01 must temporarily show only the verified merge/follow-up
and explicitly advertise only that capability. Do not use mock results to fill the gap.

## Script 1: one instruction, approximately 20 seconds
0–3s: Screen shows three synthetic PDFs. Caption: “This PDF job should be one sentence.”
3–6s: Drag them into atepdf. Caption: “Add your files.”
6–10s: Type: “Merge these, make scanned pages searchable and target under 5 MB.”
10–14s: Show real engine progress, edited for length only with a visible disclosure.
14–17s: Open actual output. Show real measured size, retained page count and a real text search.
17–20s: “Drop it. Tell it. Done.” CTA: “Try with your files.”
Privacy note: “PDFs processed on your device. AI interprets your instructions online.”
Do not type a fake target result into the output card; use the capture generated by the actual build.

Primary ad text:
“Combine, make searchable and compress PDFs in one workspace. Add your files, describe the job,
then preview the result. PDF processing happens on your device; cloud AI interprets your instructions.
Start with the free allowance.”

Headline: “One instruction. One finished PDF.”
Description: “Try the real workflow.”
No quantified speed or time-saving claim is authorised.

## Script 2: document-pack frustration, approximately 15–20 seconds
0–3s: Three attachments labelled “Brief”, “Appendix” and “Scan”, all synthetic.
Caption: “One document pack. Not three separate jobs.”
3–6s: Show correct file order and one instruction.
6–12s: Real local execution; show caption “Footage shortened” when cutting elapsed time.
12–16s: Preview combined output, find the appendix page, show available save/share controls.
16–20s: “Get the PDF you meant to send.” CTA: “Try with your files.”
Avoid drawing a fake competitor screen or claiming a competitor takes a particular number of clicks.
If a comparison is later made, record the exact version and a reproducible test using equivalent inputs.

## Script 3: founder-led local-processing explanation, approximately 25 seconds
Spoken opening:
“AI can understand what you want without us sending your PDF to a processing server.”
Show: one real imported sample, the instruction composer, the result.
Spoken clarification:
“atepdf sends the instruction and limited file details to the AI. The PDF processing happens here,
on your device. Add your files, say what you need, and preview the result.”
CTA:
“Try the free workflow.”
This must be Shrey's genuine recording or a clearly labelled demonstrator; do not synthesize a customer endorsement.
Do not say AI is fully offline or that no data ever leaves the device.

## Research protocol
Use public sources and approved Appllama access.
Record source URL, date, screen/ad ID, what was actually seen, inferred mechanism, possible adaptation and rights.
Do not infer conversion performance from app revenue estimates, number of screens or an ad's apparent longevity.
Never paste someone else's testimonial or performance metric into atepdf copy.
The prior Appllama material was metadata-based; obtain actual screen access before any visual-copy analysis.

## Originality and rights
Borrow a structure such as problem/demo/result, not distinctive footage, branding, reviews or likeness.
Creators receive written scope: deliverables, truthful claims, duration of paid usage, territories,
editing permission, raw-file access, disclosure responsibility, compensation and termination.
No use of music or stock assets without the relevant commercial licence.
Keep receipts/licences in the private operating records; do not distribute third-party font files.

## Production checks
- Short opening, readable UI, focus on a specific use case.
- Synthetic content only; no account details or credentials in the recording.
- Actual operation and result; no invented progress or fake saved confirmation.
- Supported platform clearly visible. Browser advert should not imply native app behaviour.
- Accurate on-device processing statement and online-instruction qualification.
- Price/subscription terms match actual checkout and current release.
- Subtitles, audio clarity, contrast and placement-safe crops.
- Check 9:16 and 4:5 exports in real placement previews before publication.
- Default automatic claim/copy/video transformations off unless reviewed.
- One primary CTA. No fake countdown or unverified user count.

## Naming and iteration
File: APDF_US_WEB_META_A01_H01_V01_9x16.mp4
Campaign taxonomy: product_country_surface_channel_objective_stage_version.
Concept and hook IDs remain separate.
Track when a creative or landing changed; do not compare unequal cohorts as an A/B test.
Keep at most one or two challengers beside the control while the budget is small.

## Reference-to-outcome loop
Reference -> observed mechanism -> original hypothesis -> claim proof -> asset -> matched page
-> activated cohort -> first payments -> refunds/repeat use -> keep, change or reject.
The durable asset is this learning record, not the volume of generated videos.
