# Landing, conversion and lifecycle copy
Draft copy only. Claims depend on the verified capability registry and legal/privacy review.

## Baseline /document-pack
Headline: **One instruction. One finished PDF.**
Subhead: **Combine files, make scans searchable and reduce the result's size without jumping between tools.**
Primary CTA: **Try with my files**
Secondary CTA: **See a sample**
Supporting line: **PDF processing happens on your device. Cloud AI helps understand your instructions.**

Proof: a short real workflow with synthetic files. Captions show input, instruction and actual result.
Below proof:
1. Add your files.
2. Say what you need.
3. Preview, save or ask for another change.

Paid section:
Free includes useful local tools and 3 completed AI jobs each month.
Pro adds more conversational jobs, supported larger batches and reusable workflows.
Use the actual locale-aware prices returned by the verified billing provider.
Show annual full cost, renewal terms and monthly choice. No hardcoded fictitious discount.

FAQ:
“Are my PDFs uploaded?” Document processing stays on this device. Your instruction and limited file metadata
are sent to the AI provider after permission. PDF contents are not sent for these processing operations.
“Does it work offline?” Supported local shortcuts and saved recipes can work without a model request.
New natural-language instructions require connectivity.
“Will every PDF get below 5 MB?” No. The app targets the limit and reports the actual result and trade-offs.
“Can I edit or sign?” Not in this version. It processes files; it is not a text editor or signing service.
“Will my files appear on my other devices?” No automatic document sync. Pro access can be linked across supported devices.
“Do I need to pay to get a free result?” No. Any supported paid limit is explained before a new paid job.
“Can I cancel?” Link to provider-appropriate subscription management and accurate remaining-access terms.

## /smaller-pdf variation
Headline: **A smaller PDF, without the settings maze.**
Subhead: **Tell atepdf the size you are targeting. It processes the file locally and shows the actual result.**
CTA: **Try with my PDF**
Do not promise guaranteed size, unchanged quality, perfect preservation or universal document support.
Reuse the same page framework; keep the price and free policy unchanged during initial creative tests.

## /searchable-pdf variation
Headline: **Make your scan searchable.**
Subhead: **Add a scanned PDF, ask for searchable text, and check the result in the same workspace.**
CTA: **Try with a scan**
Display supported language/capability limits for the current platform.
Show an actual search in an independently reopened output, not just text shown in a chat reply.

## /private-pdf-processing variation
Headline: **Your device does the PDF work.**
Subhead: **AI interprets the instruction. Local engines process your documents.**
CTA: **Try the workflow**
Do not say no data is uploaded: the instruction is transmitted.
No security certifications, “bank-grade” claims or guaranteed provider nonretention without evidence.

## In-product offer
Title: **Make repeat PDF jobs easier.**
Three benefits: more AI-planned jobs; supported larger batches; save and rerun recipes.
Annual and monthly choices, full billing period and localized price.
Primary: **Get Pro**
Secondary: **Continue free**
Utility: **Restore purchases**
Keep job/files and old output accessible on dismissal. Never inject a marketing pixel into the workspace.

## Optional lifecycle emails
Only send marketing messages with the required consent and working unsubscribe.
Operational purchase receipts and billing help are separate from marketing consent.
Do not refer to document names, page text or raw instructions.

Email 1, after an opted-in account is created:
Subject: “Your next PDF job can start with one sentence”
Body: “Add your files, tell atepdf the result you need, and preview the output. Start with the sample
document pack to see how it works. Your PDFs are processed on your device; AI interprets the instruction online.”
CTA: “Try the sample”
Do not send to someone who only used the app anonymously.

Email 2, after useful repeat work and explicit marketing permission:
Subject: “Doing the same PDF job again?”
Body: “A saved recipe keeps the steps together, so you can reuse them on your next set of files.
Open atepdf to try it on a new batch. Pro is available for repeated workflows.”
CTA: “Open atepdf”
Send only when the feature is supported. Do not fabricate a personal usage statistic.

Email 3, one later return invitation, not an indefinite sequence:
Subject: “A document pack, without the tool-switching”
Body: “When the next batch arrives, add it to atepdf and say what you need. The same workspace handles
the supported steps and returns a file you can preview.”
CTA: “Prepare a document pack”
Suppress after unsubscribe, purchase where irrelevant, complaint or consent withdrawal.

## Creator outreach
Subject: “A PDF workflow your audience can try”
“Hi [name], I am building atepdf: add files, describe the job, and get a processed result without
working through separate tool screens. The PDF processing runs on the device.

Your [specific relevant demonstration] seems a good fit. Would you be open to testing a working build
and, if useful, discussing a disclosed demo with paid usage rights? Here is a synthetic-file walkthrough.
No positive review is required.”

Do not send in bulk or invent familiarity. Research and approve each recipient.

## Customer interview prompts
“What were you trying to send or submit?”
“What did you use last time?”
“Where did you get stuck?”
“Would you do this again in the next month?”
“What would justify paying for it?”
“What would make you stop trusting it?”
Never ask participants to send confidential source documents to our backend for this marketing research.
