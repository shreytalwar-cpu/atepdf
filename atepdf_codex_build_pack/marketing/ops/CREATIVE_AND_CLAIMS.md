# Original creative factory and claims register
This completes PM07, PM08 and PM13; it does not add another production platform.

## One identity from research to revenue
Use a stable internal ID: `APDF-US-WEB-PACK-C01-E01-H01-V01`.
C = concept, E = execution, H = hook, V = revision. Never rename a published version to hide a change. Record its asset hash, source build, page variant, preset, channel and experiment ID. Use safe IDs, never customer names or filenames, in URLs and advertising identifiers.

Use [asset-record.json](templates/asset-record.json) and [claims-register.json](templates/claims-register.json). All seeded claims remain unverified until application evidence exists.

## Minimum asset package
One source recording with synthetic documents; 9:16 and 4:5 target cuts; accessible captions; a clean thumbnail; script; measured timings/file sizes; rights record; review decision. Verify current placement previews rather than hardcode old safe-zone assumptions. No font binaries in this handoff.

The working app, not an image mock-up, supplies every process/result frame. Record a build ID and actual input set. A sample is labelled as a sample. Speed-ups must be labelled. Mask test secrets, notifications and private desktop content before publishing. Licence music, voice and any stock material for the intended paid use; do not assume a social-library track is cleared for advertisements.

## First batch
Use existing concept briefs, not a new brainstorming cycle:
1. One instruction instead of switching PDF tools.
2. Three attachments become one searchable document pack.
3. Document processing stays on the user's device, with the cloud-instruction qualifier.

Only produce the OCR/compression sequence when it passes the target platform's checks. A real merge-only ad is better than a beautiful fictional demo. Keep the call to action matched to the same actual preset/page.

## Claim types and proof
| Claim | Required proof before publication |
|---|---|
| Processed on this device | Current target-platform network audit and no remote operation fallback |
| Make scans searchable | Independent text/search checks on the displayed file |
| File reduced to a shown size | Real before/after bytes and readable preview on the displayed example |
| Saves a quantified amount of time | Documented comparable baseline, sample size, measurement and limitations |
| Works on a named platform | Released/tested build for that platform |
| Price or saving | Current approved provider offer; full billing period and conditions |
| Customer quote | Genuine quote, permission, context and material-relationship disclosure |

Do not use “nothing ever leaves your device”, “fully offline AI”, “guaranteed under 5 MB”, “all PDFs”, “best”, “first” or “Smallpdf killer” as factual public claims. A competitor comparison requires dated equivalent tasks and capabilities, not an invented feature grid.

## Approval and expiry
Approval binds the exact asset hash, claim IDs, price version, landing version, channel/account and permitted date range. A changed price, claim, asset, landing page, expired licence or broken feature invalidates the relevant approval. Record a new review; do not silently reuse the old one. Templates are not legal releases or actual approvals.

## Avoid false learning
A colour change is not a new concept. Organic views are not paid conversions. Ad-library longevity is not proof of profit. Retain unsuccessful creatives and the reason for changing them, without labelling sparse evidence a statistically certain winner.

Founder approval is required for paid/public material. The FTC guidance on material connections and genuine experience informs creator copy; jurisdiction-specific release review remains necessary. [T17]
