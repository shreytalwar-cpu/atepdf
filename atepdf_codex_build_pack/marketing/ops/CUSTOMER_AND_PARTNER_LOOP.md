# Learn from buyers, then earn distribution
This is the practical feedback loop behind the Rohan-inspired strategy. It does not import his claimed CAC or revenue, and does not add a new concierge extraction product.

## First ten conversations
Recruit relevant people through individually reviewed channels. Ask for permission to observe a task, using a harmless sample or their own private device. Do not collect PDFs for research.

Ask:
1. What document task did you last do, and why did the final file matter?
2. What steps and tools did you actually use?
3. Where did you hesitate or repeat work?
4. How often has the same task happened recently?
5. What did you try before paying for any tool?
6. Can you complete this task here without instructions from me?
7. What would stop you using it next time?
8. After seeing the exact paid offer, what would you choose and why?

Do not ask “Would you pay for this amazing AI app?” Record observed behaviour separately from stated intention. Friends, founder-led pilots and cold paid buyers are different cohorts.

## Objections that become product or creative work
| Objection | Response/work |
|---|---|
| “Where do my documents go?” | Clear local-processing/cloud-instruction explanation plus actual tests |
| “Why pay when merge is free elsewhere?” | Show repeat-job convenience; keep honest free access |
| “I need it only once.” | Respect the free option; record episodic need rather than push an annual plan |
| “This failed on my phone.” | Fix/delist that capability before buying traffic |
| “I need signing/Word conversion.” | Explain scope; do not promise a roadmap to win the sale |
| “Can I use my purchase elsewhere?” | Demonstrate access linking, with no implied file sync |

Store de-identified themes in [voice-of-customer.json](templates/voice-of-customer.json). Keep real contact information in the approved private system with retention controls. Public quotes require explicit permission and exact wording. A draft objection is not a customer quote.

## Small creator/partner test
Screen 5–10 relevant productivity, virtual-assistant or operator creators for audience fit, geography, comment quality, rights and truthful disclosure. Do not pay solely for follower counts. Use original product footage and a real test. Keep a record of outreach permission, offer, fee, affiliate terms, usage period and cancellation. No bulk outreach is authorised.

Starting affiliate hypothesis: around 20% of the first payment's net receipts, as defined in the existing plan. Show upfront fees, taxes and commission together in CAC. Reconcile refunds/chargebacks before payout, block self-referrals and duplicate claims, and do not create a referral-rewards product until there is demand. The commercial agreement needs review; the brief is not a contract. [T17]

Draft outreach:
“Hi [name], your work on [specific public topic] looks relevant to a PDF utility I am building. It lets people describe a document job and processes the files on their device. May I send you a short working demo? We would agree any paid usage or sponsorship separately.”

## Case-study process
Choose a genuine user who has completed a repeat task. Record the baseline, actual workflow, device, files' general category without sensitive detail, corrections and limits. Obtain approval for every identifying detail or quote. Publish only a measured result with its context. Never trade rewards for positive reviews.

## Weekly voice-of-customer pass
Review five recent de-identified observations or all available if fewer. Pick one objection to address in copy, help or product. Update the shared context and evidence register. Sparse observations guide the next experiment; they do not prove the market size.
