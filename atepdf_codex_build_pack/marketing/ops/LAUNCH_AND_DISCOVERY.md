# Launch, app-store discovery and useful search pages
The missing work is a reliable public entrance, not more acquisition channels. Keep the first spend on the existing one-market plan.

## Gate sequence
**L0: Build proof.** Core operation and recovery work on the advertised target.
**L1: Message proof.** Real demo, approved claims, rights and matched page exist.
**L2: Funnel proof.** Consent, free result, billing, restore and purchase attribution pass.
**L3: Owner authority.** Actual seller/accounts, price, campaign, total cash cap and dates are approved.
**L4: Review.** Results reconcile; the next tranche is a new decision.

Fill [launch-checklist.json](templates/launch-checklist.json). Every condition starts false, with evidence absent. Completing a checklist is not a provider-side security or spending limit.

## Preflight people will actually notice
Test iPhone Safari, Android Chrome, desktop browsers and the in-app browser reached by the advertisement. Check import, large-file handling, permission refusal, keyboard, processing, checkout return, purchase pending, save cancellation and a second request. Provide an honest “open in your browser” route when needed; never pretend local file handles transfer across devices/browsers. Test blocked cookies and no marketing consent. A rejected analytics permission must not stop a free PDF job.

Check landing speed on a realistic connection, readable video captions, visible paid terms, broken links and a working support inbox. Stage/test pages remain noindex. The app workspace must not leak file state into crawlable URLs or social previews.

## Store listing kit
Prepare drafts only: title/subtitle/description, five screenshot briefs, short preview, support/privacy links, tested-platform matrix and feature-specific entry routes. Proposed screenshot story:
1. Add files and give one instruction.
2. Combine a document pack.
3. Make a scan searchable.
4. See a verified result and continue naturally.
5. Explain local processing and cloud instruction interpretation clearly.

Do not publish unsupported platform badges or imply the Mac app is merely the web page. Prices/ratings must be real. Screenshot dimensions, text limits, eligible experiments and review requirements must be rechecked in the current console.

Apple custom product pages can support message-matched destinations; Google Play offers listing experiments. Use them after a baseline listing and purchase path work, not as a reason to create dozens of variants. Their public examples do not establish our conversion improvement. [T15,T16]

## Search pages
Start with `/document-pack`, then add `/smaller-pdf`, `/searchable-pdf` and `/private-pdf-processing` only as their capabilities and traffic justify. Extend to 6–10 useful task pages over time, not hundreds of thin variants. Each contains a real example, limits, price/free-tier explanation, brief FAQ and a safe preset link into the same workspace.

Use canonical URLs, metadata, sitemap and appropriate truthful structured data where applicable. No invented reviews, misleading rich-result markup, keyword stuffing or guaranteed rankings. Verify indexing through the actual site's Search Console when available. Search-console tooling is analytics, not proof of ranking or customer acquisition.

## Public launch kit, optional after product readiness
One product description, one real demo, original approved assets, correct download links, support/contact information, a fact sheet and a concise founder story. Keep an outreach record and obtain permission for community posts. A community launch or Product Hunt submission is optional; no bought upvotes, fake activity, mass DMs or fixed revenue promise. Do not spend a week on a press kit before the first file workflow works.

## Release and rollback
Publish only supported destinations. Roll back the bad landing/creative, stop affected delivery through approved account controls, restore the previous safe version and record the incident. Removing an ad is different from deleting customer subscriptions. Follow [AUTHORITY.md](AUTHORITY.md).
