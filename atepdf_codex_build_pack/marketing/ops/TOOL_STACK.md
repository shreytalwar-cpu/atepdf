## Accountable sources of truth
Git is the source of approved briefs and configuration. The local app controls document results. The payment ledger controls money. PostHog controls behavioural observations. Ad reports are attribution estimates. Buffer controls its queue, not ad spending. Remotion composes footage; it does not certify product performance.

## What we deliberately do not add
No duplicate scheduler: Buffer first, Postiz only for a measured gap and approval. No second ad-reporting connector beside Windsor without a reason. Canva/Figma can refine original assets but are not compulsory production steps. No extra AI-avatar, video-generation, Semrush/Ahrefs, Superly-like workspace, n8n or BI purchase at launch.

The operational asset library lives in the repository. Sensitive feedback and credentials do not. Marketing accounts must never receive customer PDFs to help generate creative.

## Cost policy
INR 2,000–4,000/month is a proposed incremental marketing-software cap, **not current authorisation or a quote for the complete stack**. Begin at zero new commitments and use existing/free eligible access. Allocate any actual expenditure once to an approved creative/research or operating bucket; do not add it on top of all previous caps or double-count it in CAC.

No third-party free tier or licence is guaranteed. Verify account eligibility, current plans, usage and organisation-level licence terms, especially for Remotion. Do not ship proprietary third-party art or font files as part of the handoff.

## Ownership and security
Use the chosen legal seller/brand accounts, MFA and scoped roles. Do not mix atepdf data with jewellery-store campaigns. Start read-only where provider enforcement permits. A full-access OAuth token plus a prose rule is not a technical read-only boundary. Tool allowlists, provider roles and approval settings need separate verification. No secrets in the repo, screenshots, CLI output or support logs.
