# Primary policy and lifecycle references

These current documents support implementation checks, not a blanket compliance certification. Recheck before release, particularly billing region, privacy disclosure and account-deletion requirements.

## P01 | Apple App Review Guidelines
Checked 2026-09-28. https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/

Privacy/minimal selected access, explicit permission for third-party AI sharing, account deletion, system review requests and applicable purchasing rules. Recheck exact scope on submission.

## P02 | Google Play subscriptions policy
Checked 2026-09-28. https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9900533?hl=en

Clear billing/trial terms and discoverable management/cancellation. Regional exceptions and actual product terms remain relevant.

## P03 | RevenueCat restoring purchases
Checked 2026-09-28. https://www.revenuecat.com/docs/getting-started/restoring-purchases

User-triggered restore and account transfer/alias handling; not proof that RevenueCat is already integrated.

## P04 | Google Play subscription lifecycle
Checked 2026-09-28. https://developer.android.com/google/play/billing/lifecycle/subscriptions

Cancellation while still entitled, revocation, grace/hold and provider-backed state reconciliation.
