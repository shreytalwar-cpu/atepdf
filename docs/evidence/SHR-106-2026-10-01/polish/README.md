- more brand expression and a black app icon;
- a motion system;
- a calmer, Codex-like conversation, with no "Planning";
- real progress;
- Settings polish.

- [`after/`](after/): the real Mac Release app, synthetic container (`dev.atepdf.synthetic.mac`), sample PDFs only.
- [`../web/`](../web/): the web state harness, regenerated by `ui-states.spec.ts`.

## Fixes made after the Mac captures

The `after/` Mac captures were taken from the first polish build. The walkthrough found these issues, which were then fixed in code. The fixes are verified by the web harness and the type and e2e runs, and the final Mac Release build compiles them, but they were not re-captured on the Mac:

- **Files panel rail:** the Iris rail was painted under the highlighted row on macOS. It is now drawn above the row.
- **Progress track:** too faint in light mode. It now uses `pressed`.
- **Undo during the hold:** the previous card briefly offered Undo while the next reply was held. Hidden until the reply appears.
- **Request block:** the leading chevron was removed.
- **Examples in short windows:** "Try asking" examples were clipped under the composer in windows shorter than 720 pt. They are shown only when there is room.
